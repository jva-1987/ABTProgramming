[Ccr22x] Cooling circuit, room cooling

Name: [Ccr22x] Cooling circuit, room cooling

Library hierarchy: Plants

Plant operating modes:

  • Protection
  • Economy
  • Pre-Comfort
  • Comfort
  • Off

Plant diagram

Main functions

Main components

  • Room temperature control
  • Dew point temperature calculation and flow temperature setpoint shift
  • Pump
  • Valve
  • Flow and return temperature sensors
  • Sensor for outside temperature
  • Room temperature and humidity sensors
  • Condensation detector

Two possibilities for room temperature control

Indirect room temperature control:

Based on the room operating mode, different flow temperature setpoints are calculated and sent to the hydraulic circuit that performs the flow temperature control.

Keep the defaults: CcrPltMod22 and HydCrtC21.

Direct room temperature control:

Based on the room operating mode, different room temperature setpoints are calculated and sent to the hydraulic circuit that performs the room temperature control.

Change the alternatives: from CcrPltMod22 to CcrPltMod23 and from HydCrtC21 to HydCrtC23.
